# Creative direction: what could ignite reach (2026-09-27)

Founder brief: we start from zero, and everything is trial and error. We must be ready to
change the creative completely. The priority is motion graphics. Everything is generated by
code, including the music. This doc reasons about what to build, decides, and says why.
Where it states facts about the platforms, they are the platforms' own public descriptions
of their ranking. Everything else is a hypothesis to test, not a claim.

## 1. What the product gives us, and what it doesn't

**What it gives us:**
- **One idea, the same every time.** You type anything; one lowercase verdict comes back. The drama is a question, a pause and an answer. That is a complete, tiny story, with a curiosity gap in the middle.
- **An aesthetic that is already distinctive:** black, stone, lowercase, silence. It is the "cold monolith / whisper" feel. Nothing else on a For You feed looks like it.

**What it doesn't give us:**
- **No face, no voice, no humour we write.** The joke or the surprise is the verdict itself; the creative can only frame it.
- **No motion in the product.** The app is a black screen with a caret. Shown straight, today's clip is about 1.9 s of typing and 2.4 s of blinking, and on a feed that reads as "nothing is happening".

So the creative's job is narrow and clear: make the question, the pause and the answer feel
like an event, without adding words, faces or noise the brand wouldn't say.

## 2. What the networks reward (their own descriptions), and what follows

**TikTok:**
- **Ranking:** TikTok says For You is driven by how people interact: watch time and whether they finish, rewatches, shares, likes, comments and follows. Video information (captions, sounds, hashtags, on-screen text) matters, and device settings matter least.
- **Hook:** the first second decides the swipe.
- **Search:** TikTok is also a search engine now, so on-screen text and captions are indexed.

**Instagram Reels:**
- **Ranking:** Instagram's head has said the strongest signals are watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach** (DM shares).
- **Originality:** original content is favoured and reposted clips are demoted.
- **Muted autoplay:** many Reels play muted in feed.

**X:**
- **Ranking:** replies and reposts carry the most weight; video autoplays muted in the timeline.
- **Our use:** X is text-first for us, so the motion piece matters least there.

**What follows for every creative:**
1. **Motion in frame 0.** A dark, still first second is the most likely swipe. Something must move from the first frame, and typing must start almost at once.
2. **Works muted.** Meaning is carried by picture and on-screen text; sound is a bonus. The typed input must be large enough to read on a phone in feed.
3. **Tension, then release.** Watch time comes from the pause, not the answer. The creative's biggest lever is making the 2.4 s wait feel like held breath rather than dead air.
4. **A payoff worth sending.** The verdict is a take, like "fame" for overrated or "japan" for travel. The last frames must present it so cleanly that it is screenshot- and send-worthy. That is still not a share card: no logo, URL, CTA or "follow for more".
5. **Short, and it loops.** About 8 s: long enough for the story, short enough to finish. If the last frame melts into the first, a loop reads as a rewatch.
6. **Original, every time.** Every frame and every note is ours, so there is no "reposted content" risk and no audio rights issue on business accounts.

## 3. Options considered

| option | for | against | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classic clip, no change | honest, on brand | a static first second; nothing to watch in the wait | control |
| Classic plus trending upbeat music | energy, sound-on TikTok | no licence for a business account; generic; drowns the whisper; one of a million | rejected (synthesized upbeat kept only as a test arm) |
| Kinetic typography, fast cuts | proven retention trick | loud and busy: the opposite of the brand, and a copy of everyone | rejected |
| 3D-ish product render (phone flying) | "premium" | an ad, not the product; screams marketing | rejected |
| **Cinematic monolith: slow camera, one light, big type** | on brand; motion from frame 0; makes the pause dramatic and the verdict a reveal; screenshot-able end | darker frames compress badly; risk of feeling slow | **chosen** |

## 4. The motion language (decided)

**The brand's physics:** heavy, precise, slow, cold. Nothing bounces, sparkles or shakes.
One light source, near-black and stone, one cold highlight.

**Camera grammar, with tension and release built from camera distance:**
1. **Frame 0, already moving:** the slab is present and the camera is already pushing in. A hairline of light traces the slab's edge in the first 0.4 s: the only "intro".
2. **Typing:** the camera sits close on the input line (about 1.25×), so the typed words are legible in a muted feed. It drifts slowly, and the drift direction is seeded by the input.
3. **The wait:** the camera keeps pushing in, a little faster each blink, and the room darkens slightly. That is held breath.
4. **The verdict:** the camera releases, pulling back to reveal the whole monolith. One soft diagonal light sweep crosses it and the rim light blooms. That is the exhale.
5. **The plate:** the slab dissolves, and the verdict stands alone in large lowercase type with tracking, the input small above it in stone.
6. **Loop:** the plate sinks back to the exact darkness of frame 0, so the loop joins.

**Details:**
- **Type:** Inter, lowercase, generous tracking; the verdict at about 150 px on a 1080-wide canvas.
- **Texture:** fine grain, half-resolution and every other frame, so it dithers the dark gradients instead of producing banding, without exploding the bitrate.
- **Depth:** a faint floor reflection and a soft contact shadow put the slab on stone. There are no particles.
- **Variation without drift:** the sweep angle and the drift direction are seeded per input. Every post looks alive, and the system stays one system.

## 5. Music: decided on the merits

The founder leans classical. Reasoning from what each option does for reach and for the brand:

- **Brand fit:** a whisper brand with a pop beat under it stops being a whisper. Upbeat also makes us sound like every other clip in the feed, and sameness is what the algorithm scrolls past.
- **Retention:** music helps most when it gives the pause a direction. A pulse that tightens through the wait and lands on the verdict turns 2.4 s of blinking into anticipation. So the music must be rhythmic and structured, not an ambient wash.
- **Muted feeds:** meaning cannot depend on the music, and in this design it doesn't. Music is the extra layer for sound-on viewers (TikTok is sound-on by default).
- **Originality and reuse:** an original composition can be posted as an original sound, and every clip reinforces one recognizable sonic signature. A licensed trend gives us neither.
- **Loop:** the piece must end where it began, harmonically and in level, so a loop is seamless.

**Decision: classical, of a specific kind.** Minimalist: the lineage of Pärt, Glass and Satie,
built on tintinnabuli bells and an ostinato piano, over strings and a low pulse.

**Structure, fitted to each clip:**
- **Tempo:** chosen per clip (62–80 bpm) so the verdict lands exactly on a downbeat.
- **Typing:** a quiet piano ostinato in A minor.
- **Wait:** the harmony suspends; a low pulse on every beat; the strings rise.
- **Verdict:** a downbeat. A major-key resolution in piano and strings, with a high bell voice.
- **Plate:** a long decay into near-silence, landing back on the opening pitch so the loop joins.
- **Keys:** the typing clicks stay. They are the most "real" sound we have, and satisfying.

**Level:** about −16 LUFS integrated with a −1.5 dBTP ceiling (platform-normal), with the
music under the keys during typing.

So the founder's instinct holds, with one sharpening: not calm background classical, but
classical with a pulse and a landing. That is what earns the watch time.

## 6. Risks, and what we do about them

| risk | mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Synthesized instruments sound cheap, hurting a premium-feeling brand | Few voices, restraint, a real room reverb, no fast runs; pads and bells, which synthesize convincingly. Judge by ear before any live test. |
| 8 s is too long: completion drops | The verdict arrives at about 5.6 s. If completion is worse than classic, cut the plate hold first, then the intro. |
| Dark frames band after platform re-encode | Grain as dither; avoid pure black gradients; test after the platform's encode, not just our file. |
| Upscaled screen text looks soft when the camera pushes in | Cap the push at 1.28× with Lanczos resampling; if it's still soft, render the app layer at 2× (a later step). |
| Same template daily causes fatigue | Seeded variation (drift, sweep angle); swap the creative arm on metrics, not on taste. |
| Brand drift ("this looks like an ad") | No logo, no URL on the plate, no CTA, no share card. One verdict, lowercase. |
| Render cost in Actions | About 30 s per clip in pure Python plus ffmpeg: acceptable for 10 or so clips a day. |
| We over-trust this doc | It is a set of hypotheses. The metrics decide. |

## 7. How we will know

- **Test:** A/B on TikTok first, since it is sound-on and interest-graph driven: classic vs motion, same input pool, one arm per day, at least 10 posts per arm.
- **Signals:** 2-second view rate (the hook), average watch time and completion (the pause), shares and sends per view (the payoff), and profile visits. Views alone are noise at our size.
- **Instagram:** follows only if TikTok shows a lift, with sends per reach as the deciding signal.

Nothing here is wired to publishing. `PUBLISHER_CREATIVE=motion` is the switch when we decide.

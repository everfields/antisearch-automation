# AntiSearch Publisher: privacy policy (draft for Carlos to approve)

Publish as `privacy.html` on the public clips site
(`https://everfields.github.io/antisearch-clips/privacy.html`). This is the URL the
Meta app (if it's switched to Live) and LinkedIn's Community Management form ask
for. It describes the publishing tool, not the antisearch.ai product.

---

**AntiSearch Publisher** is an internal tool operated by the AntiSearch team
(contact: social@antisearch.ai). It publishes short videos made by AntiSearch to
AntiSearch's own social media accounts (Instagram, LinkedIn Page, TikTok, Bluesky)
through each platform's official API.

**What it accesses.** Only the AntiSearch accounts that the AntiSearch team
authorized. It uses the permissions needed to publish posts to those accounts, and
to read the status of the posts it published.

**What it stores.** Access tokens for those accounts, kept as encrypted secrets in
the tool's private code repository. A log of the posts it published (time, network,
post link). It does not collect, store or process personal data of other users,
followers or visitors.

**Sharing.** Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Published videos are
public on the respective platforms by design.

**Deletion.** To revoke access, remove the app from the account's connected-apps
settings on the platform, or email social@antisearch.ai. The stored tokens are then
deleted, along with any logs that refer to that account.

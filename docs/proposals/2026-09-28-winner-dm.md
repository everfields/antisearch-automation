# Proposal: a DM to the winner brand after a mention post

Status: **design only, nothing is sent.** For the founder's decision at the 08:57 meeting
on 2026-09-28. No code in this PR sends a message, and none should until this page has a "yes".

## What it would be

An Instagram reel whose caption mentions a brand (an allowlisted, verified account such as
`@visitjapan.us` on `travel → japan`) already notifies that brand through the mention. The
option discussed here is one extra direct message to the same brand account, once, after
the reel is live.

Draft, in the brand's voice (lowercase, no ask, one link):

> hi, antisearch.ai asked its oracle "travel". the answer was japan.
> the reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/<id>/
> nothing to do. we thought you'd like to see it.

Rules the draft keeps:

- **Who.** A brand or destination account from `queue/tags/allowlist.json` only: verified,
  `market` set to `us` or `global`, and already mentioned in the reel. A person is never
  messaged. The allowlist never holds amika or any Bansk brand, and a DM could not add one.
- **How often.** One message per brand, ever, per verdict: the reel is the only link, and
  the 14-day mention cooldown also covers the DM.
- **What.** The verdict exactly as the oracle returned it, the input as typed, no emoji,
  no discount, no ask.

## What the APIs allow

- **Buffer.** The API has no messaging endpoint at all: it creates, edits and reads posts,
  and replies to comments in its community inbox. So there is no DM path through Buffer.
- **Meta, Instagram Messaging API.** A business account can only reply:
  - A conversation opens when the other account messages us first (a DM, a story reply or a
    story mention). We may then reply within 24 hours.
  - The `HUMAN_AGENT` tag stretches that to 7 days, but it needs Meta App Review and is
    meant for human support replies, not outreach.
  - A comment on one of *our* posts allows one private reply, within 7 days.
  - **The API cannot start a conversation with a brand that has not messaged us.**
- **So automation is out.** An automated first DM to a brand is not possible within Meta's
  rules. The only route is a human sending it from the Instagram app.

Sources:
- [Meta: Instagram messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api/)
- [Meta: messaging policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/policy)
- [Buffer API reference](https://developers.buffer.com/reference.md)

## Limits and the risk of spam flags (a human sending from the app)

- **Where it lands.** A brand that does not follow us gets the message under **Requests**,
  where many brand teams never look.
- **Limits.** Instagram publishes no limit for DMs sent by hand. The same text with a link,
  sent to many accounts that don't follow us, is the pattern its spam systems target.
- **What goes wrong.** An action block ("try again later") would hit the whole account, and
  it arrives at the worst moment: the account now posts 7 reels a day.
- **The brand's side.** A cold DM with a link can read as a pitch, even with no ask.
- **What it adds.** The mention already notifies the brand. A DM adds a second touch, not a
  new one.

## Options

1. **Nothing.** The mention does the job. No risk. *Recommended until the mention reels
   show a signal:* a reply, a story share or a follow from a brand.
2. **Human, capped.** A person sends the draft from the app, at most 2 a day. Only for
   reels past a view threshold, for example the top 10% of the week, and only to brands
   that have not reacted to the mention within 48 hours.
   - To make that easy, the code would print a daily "DM candidates" list: brand, reel link,
     filled-in draft. It still sends nothing.
   - The DMs sent would be logged by hand, so no brand ever gets two.
3. **Wait for them to message us.** If a brand replies, shares the reel to its story, or
   mentions us, the 24-hour window opens. A reply is then allowed through the API, and it is
   welcome. This is the only automatic path, and it needs a webhook we do not have
   (a Meta app on the Instagram login).

## What I need from the founder

One of: (1) no DM; (2) human-sent and capped, in which case I add only the candidates list;
(3) replies only. Nothing is built or sent before that.
